🌌 Universe One — Classroom Adventure

Experimental classroom game for ordinary browsers and optional Meta Quest VR over HTTPS. No student login, microphone, camera, multiplayer, or personal-data collection. Sample mission completion is saved locally when browser storage is available. Teacher supervision required. Do not use as a production classroom.

Play without VR

WASD or arrow keys to move, or use the buttons below. In the hallway, approach a door to select its classroom. VR is optional.

Sample mission progress is stored only in this browser when storage is available. No account or cross-device synchronization.

Classroom station

Left trigger toggles the hallway; grip cycles rooms or answers; right trigger enters a room or submits an answer. Look around naturally in VR. Optional movement: use the left controller thumbstick to walk along the central aisle. Release the stick to stop. Remain seated if preferred; stop immediately if uncomfortable.
