Classroom station

Aim your Quest controller at one of the three learning objects and press the trigger to select it. The left object explores space, the center explores mathematics, and the right explores biology. In a regular browser, select a station and use the interaction button. Look around naturally in VR. For seated comfort, the scene is positioned in front of the learner and does not move the camera.
