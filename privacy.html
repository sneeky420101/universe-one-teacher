Privacy notice — educational preview

Effective November 21, 2026. Project: Universe One AI Teacher, VOLTFRAME Studios.

What this preview does

This preview provides sample educational lessons, quizzes, and an experimental VR classroom. It does not offer student accounts, chat between users, or a generative AI service. The lesson search processes the question in your browser against the included sample lessons.

Information stored on your device

Quiz completion is saved in your browser's local storage under universe-one-progress, where storage is available. This information is not intentionally sent to the app's server by the preview code. You can clear it by clearing site data in your browser. Offline assets may be stored in a browser service-worker cache.

Website hosting and external links

The hosting provider may process IP addresses, request details, and technical logs to deliver the site. This notice does not claim that hosting providers collect no information. Links to third-party educational sources have their own privacy practices; opening them leaves this preview.

Children and supervision

The preview is intended for supervised educational evaluation. Do not enter names, contact information, school records, passwords, or other personal information into the lesson search. This is not an approved school information system or a production child-directed service.

VR permissions

The experimental Meta Quest lab requests an immersive VR session if you select Enter VR on a supported device. The preview does not intentionally request microphone, camera, or location access. VR platform/browser providers may handle device and sensor data under their own terms.

Future changes

Accounts, analytics, payments, cloud progress synchronization, and AI tutoring are not part of this preview. Any future features that collect personal information require an updated notice, appropriate consent and safety review before launch.

Contact and corrections

For privacy requests, contact VOLTFRAME Studios through the official project or store contact channel once published. A verified contact address has not yet been configured; this must be completed before public store submission.

This notice describes the repository preview as currently designed and must be checked against actual hosting configuration and third-party services before release.
