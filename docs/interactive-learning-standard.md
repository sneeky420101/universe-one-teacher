# VOLTFRAME Universe One AI Teacher — Interactive Learning Standard
Status: engineering specification, not a claim of implemented features.
Copyright © 2026 Brandon Hastings — VOLTFRAME Studios.

## Core principle
Mistakes are learning signals, not disciplinary offenses. Feedback must describe the specific action and offer an achievable next step without shaming, ridicule, or public comparison.

## Lesson contract
Each interactive lesson must define:
- learning objectives and prerequisites;
- subject, grade/competency band, language, jurisdiction and standards provenance;
- conceptual explanation, vocabulary, visual demonstrations and step-by-step procedure;
- manipulable objects and expected actions, with keyboard/touch/gamepad/VR equivalents;
- common misconceptions, error detection, hints, remediation and retry paths;
- assessment rubric and mastery evidence;
- accessibility alternatives and safe exit/pause/resume;
- references, licensing, educator review and version history.

## Correction engine
1. Detect the specific incorrect action or misconception, with confidence and without guessing motives.
2. Stop or pause dangerous simulation actions; do not simulate hazardous physical instruction beyond safe educational boundaries.
3. Describe what happened neutrally, explain why it matters, and show the expected outcome.
4. Offer a progressively more explicit hint, optional demonstration, or prerequisite refresher.
5. Permit unlimited practice retries; never shame, insult, or penalize a learner for seeking help.
6. Reassess mastery using a comparable but not identical task.
7. Provide educators private, factual progress reports without punitive labels.

Example:
- Avoid: "Wrong again. You failed."
- Use: "That connection doesn't complete the circuit yet. Check whether both terminals connect to the power source. Want to see the current path highlighted?"

## Graphics and interaction quality
Use scalable fidelity tiers, not a single mandatory high-end render setting:
- High: physically based materials, detailed meshes, spatial audio, articulated tools, close-up annotations.
- Balanced: reduced geometry and textures, equivalent instructional interactions.
- Accessible: 2D diagram, narration, captions, keyboard/touch operation and reduced-motion mode.
No lesson may depend solely on color, sound, headset ownership or expensive hardware.

## Breadth and sequencing
Build reusable workshop templates across mathematics, sciences, language, humanities, art, computing, health literacy, finance, agriculture, automotive, construction, electrical fundamentals, plumbing, HVAC, manufacturing, culinary arts and more. Expand subject coverage only with verified source and educator review; do not claim complete worldwide accreditation or universal curriculum equivalence.

## Assessment and privacy
Keep practice separate from graded assessments. Student performance must not be publicly ranked by default. Minimize personal data; require appropriate consent and school-specific privacy/security review before real student accounts.

## Definition of done per lesson
- All prerequisite and procedural steps represented
- Correct, incorrect and unsafe actions tested
- Hints and remediation available
- Screen-reader/keyboard/touch alternatives checked
- VR and non-VR learning outcomes equivalent
- Educator content and safety review completed
- Performance tested on low-end and high-end devices
- Provenance and licensing recorded
- No public release without owner approval
