# VOLTFRAME STUDIOS — private IP protection workflow

**Status: internal preparation only; not an LLC, trademark registration, copyright registration, or patent filing.**

Owner-designated parent brand: VOLTFRAME STUDIOS. Product brands remain distinct. Founder: Brandon Hastings. Reference identifier: 1788.

## Immediate no-fee safeguards
- Preserve private Git history and dated commits; keep backup exports and release hashes. Do not assume a Git commit alone proves legal ownership.
- Add accurate copyright notices to wholly owned original code and artwork, subject to contributor and third-party rights. Do not label third-party code or generated material as exclusively authored by the founder without verification.
- Keep a per-product ledger of authors, contractors, creation dates, license sources, and agreements assigning or licensing IP.
- Keep nonpublic inventions and implementation details private pending patent counsel review. Avoid public disclosure of potentially patentable details.
- Use the ™ symbol only for marks actually used to identify goods/services; do not use ® without a federal registration.
- Inventory credentials, access, and repository collaborators; enforce MFA and least-privilege access.
- Confirm trademark clearance, including USPTO federal records, state databases, web/domain results and similar-sounding marks before relying on a name. A search does not guarantee clearance.

## Applications and filing gates
- Confirm formation state, registered agent, legal address, entity ownership and exact name; Florida LLC minimum mandatory filing cost currently $125, and annual reports have recurring fees.
- Confirm mark owner, precise goods/services, filing basis, classes and specimens where applicable. Federal trademark filing requires payment; filing does not guarantee registration.
- Determine original copyrightable material and correct author/claimant for registration. Registration is optional for initial protection but materially affects enforcement of U.S. works.
- Seek patent counsel to assess novelty and filing strategy; a game idea or generic feature is not automatically patentable.
- Do not submit filings, spend money, transfer IP, publish apps or change legal ownership without specific owner approval.
